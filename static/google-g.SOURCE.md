# Google sign-in logo

`google-g.png` is the unmodified Google G asset downloaded from
<https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png> on 2026-10-18.
It represents the Google G requested for the login/register button; the large
operator-supplied PNG remains untouched in the local workspace.

The button serves this asset locally without an external image request, preserves
its aspect ratio and color in both themes, and uses an empty alternative text
because the adjacent button label already names the action.

Usage: <https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines>.
Google brand assets remain subject to their owner's branding guidelines; they
are not project artwork licensed under the repository's AGPL license.
